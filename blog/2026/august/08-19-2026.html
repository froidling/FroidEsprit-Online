Archival Mega-Upload

By VA7ECX | August 19, 2026

Minor update post here, I'm moving some things around.

I started blogging a long time ago on blog.com, a now-defunct Wordpress-based blogging platform. The site went under in 2016, and I thought I lost everything. As it turns out, my folks kept everything up to October 2013, and Internet Archive kept everything past October 2014. I've decided to put myself through the paces and transcribe everything for here. I wasn't able to keep everything, I did end up losing much of 2014, but this was never meant to be such a comprehensive archive anyway. Think I'll repost about nine or ten a day until they're all there, need to pace myself so I don't get burned out.

Am I aware of the risks? Yes, I am. It is well known that observing one's own writing inflicts a degree of pyschic damage. I find perhaps it's easier for me to do this by pretending it's the work of someone else. I can hardly call the me of the past and the me of today the same person.

Not everything will be moved over, of course. I'll be censoring names and locations for privacy, and I will not be including images. However, I will not be editing any grammatical or spelling mistakes, to preserve the integrity of the original text.

Everything will be labeled under the "legacy" tag available on the sidebar on desktop or below on mobile for your perusal.

Not that there is a good reason to read the thoughts of eight-year-old me. Well, cringe is dead and I'm still breathing. Enjoy.
