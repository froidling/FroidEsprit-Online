100 Questions for Webmasters

By VA7ECX | August 11, 2026

I've been seeing this trend a few times on the indie web, I think it's been around for a few years or so, or maybe I'm mistaken. I thought I'd give it a go myself. Credits to Mouseling for the template.


1. Please introduce yourself.
I go by a few names on the internet, but for now it's best you know me as VA7ECX, a ham radio operator and generalist hobbyist.
2. How long have you been making websites?
This incarnation of my site began in May 2026, but I've been blogging since around 2012, if memory serves me correctly. I've made a few webpages over a summer course and a high school class, but this is my first "serious" one.
3. And what got you into the hobby?
A few factors. Getting fed up with the algorithm, wanting an outlet to express my interests I have complete control over, and a YouTube video about the Indie web.
4. What kind of website are you most interested in?
The kind that is self-indulgent, turns heads, and makes anyone who looks at it go "Why the hell would you make that?". In other words, silly things.
5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?
For the most part, I wing it. I keep a few templates for posts and pages, but as of late I mostly come up with an idea and focus on that until completion. I didn't really post much last month precisely because I was ruminating on a terrible idea, and now that terrible idea has three more terrible ideas piled on top of it. When I'm ready, I just ctrl-shift-p and push to Neocities.
6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.
In terms of layout? Probably PetraPixel. Her site inspired the borders around the side widgets. I was inspired by a few others too, but I can't remember the names off the top of my head.
7. What's your favourite part about making websites?
Easily working from scratch, owning all of my code. My last two attempts at a blog were based on WordPress, which felt awful to use with a sluggish admin dashboard.
8. And the thing you struggle with the most?
Probably just thinking of stuff to write. I'm a tinkerer, not a blogger. I used to be something of a good writer, but in recent years I'm all out of ideas. List prompts like these help me, but I remember once upon a time I became reliant on them and couldn't write anything for myself.
9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?
Similar, but different. I keep different stylesheet files for most of the site and the Blog section.
10. How confident are you with CSS?
I'd call myself a beginner, but I'd like to think I'm getting good at it.
11. Do you know how to correctly use <dl>?
I didn't before doing this prompt, but now I do.
12. What is your favourite HTML element?
I fear I'm too new to this to have any favourites.
13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?
Education dictates I take a piece of paper and sketch my layout before writing a single line of code, but honestly I just set up a basic CSS grid and do what looks nice to me.
14. Do you know JavaScript?
I understand some of the concepts, but I defer to StackOverflow for actual code to start, and I let intuition fill in the gaps.
15. How about PHP?
Databases are not my forte. Ironic, considering my line of work. So no, PHP is beyond me.
16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?
Depends on what one means by theme. A topic? Not really, I mostly do "what I think is interesting". Colours? Green and grey.
17. Are you more focused on content or design?
Neither, really. If I had to say, "content", since my "design" is done, but I'm not much of an artist, so I don't do much creation either.
18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?
I do! Presently, I only own this one domain, va7ecx.ca, but I've owned a few others in the past before letting the terms expire and opting not to renew.
19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?
I like them, they're fun to look at. I've even considered doing one myself, though from my experience they haven't had much "substance" to them.
20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?
Not sure what that means, but I'm pretty sure it is.
21. What are your opinion on buttons and banners?
They're fun and I want one, but I'm not enough of an artist to make them for myself. Maybe I'll consult a friend.
22. What do you think of button walls in particular?
They're fun, but I don't think I'd ever make one. Don't even have enough sites collected for one.
23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?
Probably something similar, or that is to say, my site is already to my taste, and I wouldn't want it any other way.
24. Are you envious of other people's websites?
Oh yes, all the time. Especially the simple sites that execute interesting techniques that I can never find anywhere to fit on mine.
25. What text editor do you use?
Visual Studio Code
26. Why do you use that one?
From my experience, it's more fleshed-out than Sublime Text, I like working in something more akin to an IDE. My first website I build was in Sublime, it was a pain.
27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?
Assets are hosted on-server, it's easier that way.
28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?
I have no opinion, but personally I want to move to a self-hosted homelab system.
29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?
Neocities dashboard says 12MB, that checks out.
30. Do you keep local backups of your files?
Yes, everything is kept locally.
31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?
I'm of the belief simplicity is best, but I do quite enjoy a nice visual gimmick.
32. Do you stick to certain colours? Do you do that on purpose, or is it your subconscious?
Green and dark grey are the main two colours on my site, but it isn't intentional on my part, things just happened to line up this way. Coincidentally, my guitar case is this same colour of green.
33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?
Never, this is too much fun.
34. Do you have many webmaster friends, or is it a solitary hobby?
When I started, I was on my own, but I later learned that a few other friends in my existing circles were also webmasters, and others still started soon after me (hi Moony!).
35. Do people in your real life know about your website?
I've told a few people, including my family, but isn't it just better to have a space all my own?
36. Do you update your website very often? How often is "very often"?
I can't say I do, mostly because I don't have much to write about. Most "updates" are technical, new widgets, new scripts.
37. And the overall design, do you change that much? Why or why not?
I think my design is solid, so I'm not going to be changing it any time soon.
38. Is your website more you-focused, hobby-focused, or outside world-focused?
All of the above!
39. Do you do web design professionally?
This is purely a hobby, I don't think I ever could do this for money.
40. If not, would you like to? And if you're comfortable answering, what do you do for work?
Probably not, I don't have an eye for design. I'm in finance by trade, and I may or may not pivot to electrical engineering, but software was never in the cards for me.
41. Do you communicate with people by email very much?
I can't say I do, email is where the spam and the ads are. It's inconvenient at best.
42. Some people reject social media and use websites as a replacement. Do you keep social media outside of your website?
I have social media, but I can't say I use it very often. Maybe posting once or twice every few months.
43. How about instant messengers? Do you use a mainstream one like Discord or Telegram? Or something like Matrix? Do you avoid them?
I've found my home on Discord for the last ten years, and as for Matrix, I've been toying with it lately for something related to the site. Keep your eyes peeled!
44. Do you listen to music while you work on websites? If so, what kinds of artists?
Jamie Paige and Toby Fox, cliche though they may be. If you know those names, you know exactly what kind of person I am, that's how telling it is.
45. Do you keep everything you make on one website, or do you have more than one?
Well, this is my only site, right now at least.
46. On a similar note, do you keep to one topic on your site, or many?
This is the basket I carry my eggs in.
47. Do you present your real self, or at least try? Or do you construct a persona on purpose?
Define real self? Constructed persona? I'd like to think that the me on this site is the same me as everywhere else on the internet, but I think that me couldn't be any more different from me in real life, I'm far more introverted whenever it comes to opening my mouth. Which one is the real me? I don't know.
48. Have you ever made a good friend thanks to your website?
Maybe a few, but it's hard to tell, because they were already in my circles.
49. Are you happy with the way HTML and CSS currently work?
I like it, but I'm not experienced enough to develop real opinions.
50. What are practices that you think people should avoid?
Div soup. There is no reason everything needs to be a div. Use semantic tags, folks, it's better for accessibility and screen-readers.
51. What about under-utilised practices, or things you think people should do more?
I defer to my answer in #49, I have no opinion.
52. Do you use a lot of semantic HTML? Or are you guilty of generic structure?
I try to use as much semantic HTML as possible, I'll say that.
53. Do you consider different browsers?
I don't, but right now I don't really need to? I've never run into browser-specific features yet.
54. Speaking of, what's your preferred browser? Convince your readers why they should use it.
Firefox. Chrome sucks. Google sucks with its ad mandate. Manifest V3 sucks, killing adblock. Edge sucks too, because they're starting to get rid of adblock.
55. And what OS are you on?
Windows, through no fault of my own.
56. Do you have a strong opinion on that, or do you just happen to use it?
Believe me, if I could use Linux, I would, but my job needs Excel, and some games I play won't run on Linux due to anticheat issues. Why do single-player games need anticheat anyway?
57. Are your websites mobile-friendly?
I'd like to say it is, but more work could definitely be done.
58. What are your thoughts on autoplay?
Accessibility nightmare, but at the same time some sites greatly benefit from it, like online ARGs a friend of mine wants to build.
59. What are your thoughts on webrings? Are you in any?
They seem fun, one of my circles is deliberating creating one. I might be hosting it.
60. Do you have any web shrines? What do you like to see in that sort of page?
A few, but not as much as I'd like. I see it as just a playground to mess around in.
61. Are your websites "cliche", in your opinion?
Not really?
62. What is your ideal website? Are you striving for that, or for something else?
My ideal is a website that turns heads, and I'm ashamed to say I'm not quite there yet.
63. Are you an artist? Do you draw or design your own assets?
I am many things, an artist I am not. I do dabble in photography though.
64. What are your favourite resource sites?
No opinion on that so far.
65. Is there a habit you just can't get away from no matter how hard you try?
Not really, I don't have enough experience to develop any habits.
66. What's your biggest advice for a new webmaster?
Stop thinking, just start writing things down. This is true of any art form, but especially web design, because it's hard to conceptualize what you want your box to look like if you don't have anything to put in the box.
67. Do you keep all your styling in CSS? Or do you hard-code some?
I try to style in CSS, but I admit I have some in HTML because I was too tired to switch tabs.
68. What do you think of frameset layouts?
Never heard of it.
69. How about table-based layouts?
Sounds like a pain from the early oughts.
70. Do you subscribe to the ideas of "one-column", "two-column" and "three-column" layouts? Do you use any of these?
Well, I have three columns, but I prefer the Grid system.
71. Do you spend longer on the HTML or the CSS?
Definitely the HTML. That's where the content is, after all.
72. Have you ever made a page with no CSS? It's useful for your thoughts.
I've never tried, but I might.
73. Do you ever find yourself making layouts with nothing to put on them? Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?
The latter, I don't make new layouts without serious consideration, it's quite the time commitment.
74. Would you consider yourself a beginner? Or advanced? Somewhere in the middle?
Absolutely a beginner, if one hasn't gleaned that by now.
75. Do you have a habit of looking at the source code of websites you visit?
Not really, I prefer to eyeball things.
76. How did YOU learn how to make websites?
One part summer camp, one part high school tech class, two parts online course, six parts intuition.
77. Do you ever force elements to do things they're not supposed to?
The art of the bodge is something I have yet to master.
78. Thoughts on floating elements?
Never tried.
79. When you're sizing stuff, what do you use first? Do you use px, em, %, or something else?
Pixels and percents.
80. Do you have a favourite font?
I can't say I do. I will say that I've been using Lato since before it was everywhere.
81. Would you run a website with another person? How would that work?
Worth considering, but I'd need a reason to do so. In my circles, everyone works on their own thing.
82. Do you surf the Web to find new personal websites very often?
Maybe once or twice a month, I log into Neocities and browse the Special Sauce list.
83. Do you bookmark other people's websites? How would you feel knowing someone else bookmarked yours?
I don't really bookmark any site, nor would I feel anything about others bookmarking mine.
84. What do you want people to be most impressed with when they see your website?
"Odd combination of interests, huh?"
85. Are you interested in technology outside of websites? Do you collect?
Virtual reality, ham radio, and vocal synthesis. That's the aforementioned odd combination. I can make it work.
86. How often and for how long are you online?
Too long
87. When it comes to your website, who is your target audience?
I don't really write for a target audience, or rather my only audience is myself and my circles.
88. Have you ever been interested in XHTML?
Never heard of it.
89. Do you program in general? Have you ever written a program for use with or on your website, not counting simple JavaScript?
Nothing, really.
90. Speaking of programs that help you make websites, what do you think of static site generators (SSGs)? Have you ever used one?
They sound interesting, but I don't use them.
91. Do you keep a hitcounter? Why or why not?
I have a hitcounter because I saw others had hitcounters.
92. Do you frequent forums? Which ones?
Apart from tech subreddits? None, really.
93. Do you write your page content directly into the editor, or do you prepare it elsewhere, like a text document or a Word document?
Right in the editor!
94. Do you think you appear cool to others? A more accurate answer now: do other people ever say you're cool?
My answer: I'm probably lame. The other folk's answers: yes, apparently. That's a surprise.
95. Are you embarrassed of your old work? Have you ever deleted everything out of shame?
Am I embarrasssed of my old work? Yes. Have I deleted it? No, I haven't, not myself anyway. Instead, the host went bankrupt and closed the servers without notifying anyone.
96. Would you close down your website if you couldn't update it, or would you leave an archive?
I'd likely leave an archive, but also why would I not be able to update it? After this month, I'm keeping my code locally and I have several backups.
97. Do you reveal a lot about yourself on your website? Or are you more secretive?
I don't really reveal much, but I can't say I'm secretive, it's more than there's no reason to give too much away.
98. Are you willing to reveal who your best online friend is, and/or if they have a website?
I don't really know if I have a "best online friend". Scratch that, I have dozens, and I can't say I could choose a "best". Anyone I click with right away I bestow the honour of the title "bestie".
99. And do you optimise the images on your website?
Compressing them? Limiting their dimensions? Yes, I do.
100. We're out of time! How do you feel after answering 100 questions? ....other than exhausted.
This has been a wonderful distraction, now I can refocus on that one "head-turning" project.
