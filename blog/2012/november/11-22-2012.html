Water for Life [reupload]

By a Younger Self | November 22, 2012

Hi everybody. I went on a field trip to the HR. Macmillan Space Centre, or as I like to call it, MSC. Did you know that I have a membership there? And that on the bottom floor, well, not the bottom, but in the main lobby, there is a place called the cosmic court yard? In the court yard, there is a rocket simulator. The rocket finds an eon portal and travels back in time and goes to the past Mars. According to theory, in the past, Mars had water and maybe even life. If you are looking for life on Mars, don't expect to find Martians, instead, look for microscopic, heat giving life forms called microbes in the soil. That is what you where supposed to do in the past, Until the Yeti Crab was discovered ( see post Wild Kratts). After that, a whole new world of life was discovered. Ever since, the quest for life turned into the quest for liquid water. Scientists found out that Jupiter's moon, Europa, had oceans underneath the ice. NASA scientists are working on a drilling space probe that can drill under the ice and can turn in to a swimming probe when it gets to the ocean. It might find more life than people on Earth. Back to Mars. Did you know that when NASA sends a rocket for people to go to Mars, they will be working on taming the atmosphere, which will take thousands of years? If we all pitch in and tame Mars's atmosphere, we might have another place, in space. When we got back to school, it was an hour before it was time to go. It was a fun day. Bye.
