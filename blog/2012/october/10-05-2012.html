I like origami so much that I am an origami master. It all started when I was 5 years old. I was at grandma's house when grandma came outside with an origami book and paper. "what is origami?" I asked. Now I know ahat it means. It means the Japanese art of paper folding. Legend says that if you make a 1000 cranes you will live 1000 years. The crane is one of the most complex sculptures to make. I need as much help as I can to make 1000 cranes. I have 10 right now. Maybe you can help me. You can help by making as many as you can and comment to this blog by posting the number of cranse you have down and then I will reply by saying how many I have in total after adding up all of the cranes I have. There is a book about a young girl in Japan was about to die and she wanted to live 1000 years so she tried to make 1000 cranes. The book was called Sodako and the 1000 Paper Cranes. I read it at the school mom works in when her class was studyiing Japan. I learned that she had the atomic bomb disease and she was affected by it when she was 2 and she lived 10 more years. I recommend you read this book. Back to origami. If you regularly fold a piece of paper, it is called a valley fold. The mountain fold is the opposite of the valley folds. The hardest ones are the reverse folds and sink folds. The inside reverse fold is easer than the outside reverse fold. The hardest one of all to me is the sink fold. You have to push or sink a corner of a certain fold, the frog base into the paper. My favourite thing to make is the crane because it is a symbol of good luck. I have over 8 origami books. My favourite ones are the ones that teach us how to do the crane and the flapping bird. Speaking of the flapping bird, the origami flapping bird is very similar to the crane, The only exception is if you skip a couple steps to make the crane and you do the other parts the same. If you pull the tail of the flapping bird, the wings of the bird flap. I can't wait to teach you how to do origami. Good night.
