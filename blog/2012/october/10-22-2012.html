By a Younger Self | October 22, 2012

Yesterday I was telling you that the Connect Pro workers were here to set up the new TV. Today I got to use it for the first time. I said used, not watched because it is much more than a TV. It is a computer too. It has special 3d features even if it is just a 2d show. The main menu on the main remote is called the smart hub. It is pretty much the brain of the TV. On the smart hub there is a kids section and a fitness section and even a section called Samsung Apps where you can download applications for your television. Other icons on the smart hub are YouTube and Netflix. To get to the 3d section you have to do the smart hub in order to find it. It is a Samsung App. Today, I downloaded khan academy (see post #3) but it isn't just any khan academy, it is called the khan academy education. Dad bought a keyboard that goes with the TV. Soon I am going to be able to do my blog on it. Can you believe it. First, desktop, then laptop, then Mom's tab and now the TV (If you think me I am doing my blog on the TV, you are wrong. I am doing my blog on the desktop) Dad and Jason were watching "How to Train Your Dragon" on the smart TV, but now Dad is trying how to figure out how to hook up the wireless keyboard to the TV. It has to be some sort of sensor like the wii remote and the sensor bar which makes the cursor appear. I have to learn more about how to use the TV so I can teach Mom and Dad. For one thing I know that we can go to Samsung Apps which I told you earlier. Did you know Mom's Samsung Galaxy Tab acts like a remote to the Samsung Smart TV because they are both Samsung?
