Bee sting [reupload]

By a Younger Self | October 16, 2012

Once apon a morning, my family was having brunch, which included cinnamon rolls. And then a bee appeared out of nowhere. Dad went to the kichen and got a dish towel and used it as a fly swatter, or in this case, a bee swatter. He was standing by the fireplace whacking at the poor bee trying to be a hero. He called out. "I got it!" What he did was hit the bee straight to the other side of the room. That is Dad's side of the story, time to move to Mom's side of the story. Well in this case at the other side of the room was where Mom was sitting at the dining room table and the bee went down the front of her bathrobe, down her sleeve and you won't be or "bee" surprised, but the bee stung her. Mom's arm was huge where the the bee stung her. Dad's mouth was swelling up with laughter. Everyone in the room was laughing, well everybody except the one who got stung. Mom didn't think it was funny. When I saw her sitting at the table, I stopped laughing and wanted to know how I could help her. We put ice on Mom's arm because her arm was swelling up. Dad was rolling on the floor laughing saying, "I couldn't do that again if I tried!" Every time Dad sees a bee he tells that story. Mom is still not impressed. This is a true story that actually happened to my family. Have you ever had a bee sting before? If you had, you know what to do. Have a nice week.
