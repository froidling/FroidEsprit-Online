Welcome to Geckos 2. As you can see is the sequel of Geckos. Did you know there is a species of gecko called the flying gecko? It is a relative of the Draco lizard. They are relatives because both can glide. When a flying gecko wants to find a mate or defend a territory, it clicks it's tongue at the the top of it's mouth. A flying gecko has flaps of skin that act as parachutes that help it glide. Geckos can only glide from tree to tree when he launches from a point higher than it's destination. Even though the flying gecko can glide, most lizards, even most geckos can't glide. Some geckos can even camouflage. It's called the flat-tail leaf gecko. I think that is what it is called. To geckos Madagascar is gecko heaven because of lots of geckos live there. What I said about geckos in gecko 1 was halfway there. They have tiny hairs on gecko's feet that make electricity.
